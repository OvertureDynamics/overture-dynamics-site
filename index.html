The Game

Pillar I · World Simulation

A World That Lives

Five specialized AI agents — Economy, Weather, Ecology, Geology and Narrative — run the world continuously. No developer scripts the content. The world writes its own history.

  • Prices emerge from real supply and demand
  • Over-harvested biomes stay depleted until they recover

    Combat

    No class. No limits.

    There is no class selection. The weapon in your hand defines your identity, your abilities and your role — swap weapons, swap who you are. No tab-targeting, no cooldown rotation: skill is positioning, timing and mastery.

    Dodge-Cancel

    Any animation can be cancelled into a dodge roll — a deep movement layer that separates novice from expert.

    Interrupt Windows

    Most abilities, NPC and player, have readable windows. Spotting and punishing them is the heart of high-level play.

    Positional Play

    Attack cones, backstab multipliers and AoE shapes reward spatial awareness. Every fight is geometry.

    Stamina Pressure

    Dodges and defensive skills drain stamina. Managing it under pressure is a combat skill, not a background stat.
